\## Reflection Questions



What do you hope to learn from this internship?



I hope to learn how a growing SaaS company like \[Focus Bear](https://team.focusbear.io/onboarding) balances technical systems architecture with an empathetic, inclusive user experience. I am eager to see how the theoretical frameworks from my MBA in Information Systems translate into agile, real-world product support and operations.



What skills are you most excited to develop?



I am most excited to refine my technical troubleshooting skills within a specialized software environment and to develop expertise in writing highly accessible, neurodivergent-friendly technical documentation and support playbooks.



What do you think you can contribute to the team?



I bring a strong background in IT support and technical assistance. My core contribution will be my ability to systemize chaotic tasks, break down complex technical workflows into simple "one instruction per line" steps, and proactively identify UX friction points (such as those in the First-Time User Experience) to improve user activation and retention.



What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?



While I am highly comfortable with IT support and system evaluation, I feel less confident navigating direct software development workflows (like managing complex commits and pull requests in GitHub) and deep-level backend bug resolution. I want to build my technical confidence in these areas to better bridge the gap between user support and the development team.



What does success in this internship look like for you?



Success means leaving Focus Bear's internal support documentation and IT processes significantly more organized and streamlined than when I arrived. It also means successfully identifying and helping to resolve key friction points in the user journey, ultimately contributing to a smoother, less overwhelming experience for our neurodivergent users.
