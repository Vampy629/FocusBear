\## Research Assignment: Competitive Landscape



1\. What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?



Focus Bear occupies a unique intersection in the productivity space by combining the strict, system-level distraction blocking of apps like Cold Turkey and Freedom with the neurodivergent-friendly routine building of Tiimo and Routinery. While Freedom restricts websites, it doesn't guide positive habits. While Tiimo schedules routines, it doesn't physically lock you out of distractions. Focus Bear differentiates itself by forcing users to complete their positive habit stacks before unlocking access to distracting apps and websites, making it much harder to bypass.



2\. If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?



Balancing a hybrid work schedule across different PCs alongside demanding MBA coursework requires strict digital boundaries. I would choose Focus Bear because it enforces a start-of-day morning dashboard and routine before granting full access to my desktop or laptop. It eliminates the willpower required to start the day productively; I cannot easily bypass the block to check Discord or Spotify without completing my required focus habits first.



3\. What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?



Tiimo offers highly integrated smartwatch apps (Apple Watch and WearOS) that provide visual, at-a-glance timelines and subtle haptic nudges right on the wrist. This physical, screen-free reminder system is an excellent accessibility feature that Focus Bear currently lacks. Additionally, Freedom offers a highly streamlined, web-based dashboard for instantly managing blocklists across all devices, which feels more intuitive than navigating complex in-app settings.



4\. Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?



Focus Bear could significantly improve its initial user experience by reducing cognitive load during setup. Compared to Routinery's simple, step-by-step UI, configuring Focus Bear's routines and system permissions can cause sensory overload. Implementing a simplified setup wizard that guides the user to configure just one core focus block before exposing them to the full, dense habit library would drastically improve the onboarding flow and activation rate.
